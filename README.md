1. 💼Bank Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Bank Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard is designed to analyze customer behavior and identify factors contributing to customer attrition. The dashboard helps financial institutions understand churn patterns and develop strategies to improve customer retention.

🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts a bank’s profitability and growth. Identifying customers who are likely to leave enables proactive retention strategies and improved customer satisfaction.

🔑 Key Features
Customer segmentation based on demographics and account information
Churn analysis across age groups, gender, geography, and customer categories
Interactive filters and slicers for detailed exploration
KPI cards displaying total customers, churned customers, and churn rate
Visual identification of high-risk customer segments
🛠 Tools & Technologies
Power BI
DAX
Power Query
Data Modeling
📈 Key Insights
Identified customer groups with the highest churn rates
Analyzed the relationship between customer demographics and churn behavior
Highlighted factors influencing customer retention and attrition
💼 Business Value
Supports customer retention initiatives
Helps reduce customer acquisition costs
Enables data-driven decision-making for customer engagement strategies

🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses require actionable insights into sales trends, customer preferences, and product performance to maximize revenue and profitability.

🔑 Key Features
Sales and profit analysis
Category-wise and product-wise performance tracking
Regional sales analysis
Customer purchasing behavior insights
Interactive filters and drill-through functionality
KPI cards for revenue, profit, orders, and growth metrics
🛠 Tools & Technologies
Power BI
DAX
Power Query
Excel Dataset
📈 Key Insights
Identified top-performing products and categories
Highlighted regions contributing the highest revenue
Analyzed customer purchasing trends and seasonal patterns
Tracked profitability across different product segments
💼 Business Value

Improves sales forecasting and planning

3. 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

Restaurant businesses need to understand customer preferences, product performance, and revenue trends to improve profitability and operational efficiency.

🔑 Key Features
Revenue and order analysis
Best-selling and worst-selling pizza identification
Category-wise sales performance
Order trend analysis by time period
Interactive navigation and filtering
KPI cards for revenue, total orders, and average order value
🛠 Tools & Technologies
Power BI
DAX
Power Query
Data Visualization
📈 Key Insights
Identified top-performing pizza categories and products

💼 Business Value
